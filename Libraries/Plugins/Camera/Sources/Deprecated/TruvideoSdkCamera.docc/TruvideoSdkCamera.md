
# ``TruvideoSdkCamera``

@Metadata {
    @DisplayName("TruvideoSdkCamera")

Store and capture photos and videos using a fully integrated camera system.

The **TruvideoSdkCamera** framework provides a powerful, all-in-one camera solution that enables apps to **capture, record, and manage media efficiently**. It supports **Augmented Reality (AR), barcode scanning, and high-quality photo and video operations**, making it ideal for a wide range of applications.
